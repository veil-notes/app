Veil is a local-first encrypted notes app for Android.

Keep your notes on your device without an account or cloud sync. Notes are encrypted locally before they are written to the app's private storage.

Features:
- Markdown editor
- Local OpenPGP-based encryption
- Password-protected vault with Argon2 key derivation
- Optional biometric unlock
- Automatic locking
- Password changes and encrypted note import and export
- Offline-first design

Veil is in beta. There is no password recovery or cloud backup. Keep a separate copy of critical data.

Veil is free and open-source software licensed under GPL-3.0-only. Contributions, bug reports, tests, documentation, accessibility improvements, and translations are welcome.
